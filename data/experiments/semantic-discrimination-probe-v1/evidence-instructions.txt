
EVIDENCE RESPONSE REQUIREMENTS
Keep the existing verdict semantics and required top-level response schema unchanged. The `reason` value itself must be a compact JSON object encoded as a string, with exactly these fields: `proposition`, `sourceQuote`, `candidateQuote`, and `uncertainty`.

- `proposition`: a nonempty string naming the material proposition you assessed.
- `sourceQuote`: a short, representative, nonempty exact substring from the source that supports your assessment.
- `candidateQuote`: a short, representative exact substring from the candidate, or null only when the assessed proposition is absent from the candidate.
- `uncertainty`: a nonempty string stating your uncertainty when you are uncertain, otherwise null. If equivalence cannot be established, say so explicitly in uncertainty rather than claiming certainty. Do not invent uncertainty.

Assess the entire source and candidate, not only the quoted excerpts. Keep the encoded `reason` at most 300 characters. Add no top-level fields and return only the required response JSON. These requirements change only the evidence format, not what earns PASS, LOCAL_REPAIR, or REJECT.
