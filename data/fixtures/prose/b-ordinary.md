I finally fixed the rear brake on my bike this weekend. It had been squealing since March, and I'd assumed the pads were worn, but they still had plenty of rubber on them. The real problem was a bent rotor. Once I held a ruler against it you could see the wobble, maybe a millimetre out at the worst point.

Truing a rotor is fiddly rather than hard. You find the high spot, lever it back a fraction with an adjustable spanner, spin the wheel, and check again. I went round about a dozen times before the gap looked even. The squeal is gone, though the lever feels a little soft, so I'll probably bleed the brakes next week.

If you try this yourself, clean the rotor with alcohol afterwards. Fingerprints are enough to contaminate the pads.
