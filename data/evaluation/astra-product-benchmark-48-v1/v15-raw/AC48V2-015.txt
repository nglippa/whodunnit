Volunteer Arrival Experience

Welcome to the coffee stall! Here's how your shift works.

When you arrive, start by getting an apron. They're in the cupboard beneath the sink.

Payment handling and drink preparation are separate jobs. You won't do payments on your first shift. Instead, the shift leader will pair you with a drink preparer, who will show you the order slips and the washing station.

When you're finished, put your used apron in the laundry basket next to the cupboard. Before you leave, tell the shift leader you're going.

If you feel unwell, tell the leader right away and stop handling drinks.
