A meeting on May 21 looked at making the river archive more useful to the public. It was attended by five archive volunteers and two members of the town history group. Several proposals came out of it, but no spending decisions were made.

Photograph index: The index can currently be searched only on the computer in the reading room, so visitors must travel to the archive before learning whether a relevant photograph exists. The history group suggested putting a copy online. The volunteers pointed out that some entries include personal contact details supplied by donors, and these would have to be removed before publication.

Opening hours: The archive is open on Tuesday afternoons and the first Saturday morning of each month. Two volunteers are willing to cover an additional Thursday afternoon, but that does not establish a rota, because two people must be present whenever the reading room is open. The coordinator will ask the remaining volunteers whether anyone can regularly join a Thursday shift.

Newspaper cuttings: Several boxes have not yet been listed. A preliminary listing was proposed so future users can find out what is in them while handling is kept down. It would record dates and visible headlines without unfolding fragile pages. No one claimed this would replace conservation work or make the cuttings suitable for unrestricted use.

Next steps: The coordinator will prepare a list of index fields that could be published, gather responses about Thursday availability, and estimate the time needed for the preliminary cutting list. These will be reviewed at the June meeting.

Not decided: The group did not set a public launch date, approve purchases, or promise that the additional opening session would begin during the summer. These matters depend on the information gathered before the next discussion.
