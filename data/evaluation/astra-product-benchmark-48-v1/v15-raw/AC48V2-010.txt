Mara Venn is a stage performer based in Bellweather. Her recent work includes the role of Nell in Lantern House's production of The Quiet Orchard and a touring storytelling show for primary schools. She also runs occasional voice workshops at the neighborhood arts center.

In an interview for the center's newsletter, Mara described her approach as "leave room for the listener." That phrase also appears in the title of her introductory workshop. The workshop focuses on pacing, breath, and the difference between speaking clearly and speaking loudly.
