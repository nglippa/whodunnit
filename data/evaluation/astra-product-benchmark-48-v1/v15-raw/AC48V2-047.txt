Five stars for the executive notebook. Its rigid cover makes it a little hard to open. The pages pass ink through to the sheet underneath, so every thought gets a second copy whether I want one or not. The ribbon detached before I reached page three, so I had no marker for where I stopped. I bought it because the description promised an effortless writing experience, and that promise has not held up on my desk. I have used the notebook for a week, mostly with the same fountain pen I use in my ordinary notebook, which does not show ink on the reverse. I would not buy this one again. The shop offered a replacement, but I declined because another copy of this experience seemed unnecessary.
