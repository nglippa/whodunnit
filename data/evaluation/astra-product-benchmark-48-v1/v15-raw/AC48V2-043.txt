I used to think the sound of a train leaving was a tidy ending. Door shuts, wheels move, person gone. Then my sister moved north and I learned that departures leak into the rest of the day. A scarf stays on a chair. You make too much tea. The station receipt turns up in your pocket and suddenly the afternoon has a platform in it. This is not a sad story exactly. She likes her new town, and we talk most evenings. I like hearing about the bakery with the crooked sign and the neighbour who grows enormous beans. I just want to put something down about the odd stretch between being glad for someone and missing them. Those feelings seem quite happy to share a kitchen, even if I can't explain the seating plan.
