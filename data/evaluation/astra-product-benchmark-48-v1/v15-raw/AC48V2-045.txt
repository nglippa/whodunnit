Dear Amira, here is the handover note for the costume cupboard while I am away. The key is in the small envelope at the reception desk. It opens the cupboard only; it does not open the rehearsal room. If reception is closed, call the duty caretaker rather than asking a performer to try another key. The caretaker knows where the spare is kept and can check that it is returned.

The green rail holds costumes approved for the current production. The blue rail holds items waiting for a fitting. Please keep those groups apart, even when something on the blue rail appears to match a costume on the green one. Similar fabric does not mean the cut or size is the same. A handwritten tag reading “KEEP THE PATCH” is attached to the brown coat. The director wants that visible repair retained as part of the costume.

There are six pairs of gloves in the middle drawer. Four pairs are ready to use, and two need their seams checked. I placed the unchecked pairs in a paper bag. Nobody has reported an injury from the gloves, but the seams looked loose enough that I did not want them handed out before someone inspected them. Please ask Tomas to inspect them if he is available; otherwise they can wait.

The sewing machine was serviced last month. Its foot pedal sometimes slides on the tiled floor, so use the rubber mat beneath it. The service did not include the overlocker, which is still out of use. Both machines have their own covers, and the covers should be replaced before the cupboard is locked. The overlocker cover has a red square sewn near one corner.

Please enter every loan in the paper book, including loans to people you know. The book is the only record we currently share with reception. A photograph of a page may help you remember something, but it is not a replacement for writing down the return. If an item comes back damp, hang it away from the approved costumes and tell me when I return.

I should be back on Monday afternoon, but the train connection is uncertain. Until I confirm my arrival, continue using this arrangement. Thank you for keeping track of all the small things that make a performance possible. I have left biscuits beside the book; those need no entry.
