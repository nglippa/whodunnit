The ceramic prize rules say entrants must be “members living within twelve miles of the studio.” Noor's membership is current. She has a flat eight miles away and a room near her job twenty miles away. She stays at the room on working nights and at the flat most weekends.

The rules do not define living within the area, name a particular address record, or say whether someone can have more than one residence. So the rule as written does not give a yes or no for Noor. Her flat is inside twelve miles and her room is outside it, and nothing in the rules says which one counts. A yes or no would be a new decision, not a reading of the rule.

Last year's organiser accepted an entrant who spent the winter elsewhere, but we do not know what address or circumstances that entrant supplied, so it can't settle her case. The committee has not discussed her situation, and the entry form only asks for a contact address.

For now, the answer to Noor is that eligibility is undecided. She can't be told yes or no before preparing her photographs until this year's organiser, or the committee, rules on whether her flat counts as where she lives.
