Here's what we can say about the footbridge reopening, and what we can't.

The repair report lists the first public reopening as September 3. A notice attached to the same report says the bridge remained closed to the public until September 10. The report doesn't explain the difference. It could be different hours, different sections of the bridge, or a reopening that was later reversed. We don't know which.

The delay is explained two ways. The contractor's summary says it was a missing inspection signature. The council's summary says the signature was already on file and the delay came from incomplete barrier removal. Neither summary includes the inspection sheet or the barrier removal log, so we can't check either claim, and we can't say who was responsible.

Two members of the group remember different things. One crossed on September 5. Another found the east entrance locked that evening. Both could be right, but the material doesn't show why they differ.

So the bridge's status between September 3 and September 10 is unclear, and so is the cause of the delay. Until the inspection sheet and the barrier removal log are available, we can't settle either question.
