It is worth noting that I finally glued up the walnut tabletop last weekend. Five boards, edge to edge, and I'd been putting it off since the wood arrived in March — mostly because the widest board had a slight cup I didn't trust.

Furthermore, I planed the cup out by hand. It took an hour. Maybe more. The trick is to work across the grain first, then clean up with the grain, and check with a straightedge every few passes instead of trusting your eye.

Cauls did the rest. Six of them, waxed so the glue wouldn't stick, clamped across the top to keep everything flat. When I pulled the clamps on Sunday the joints were almost invisible. Almost. There's one line near the end where the grain doesn't quite match, and I'll probably see it every time I eat breakfast.

In conclusion, the tabletop is flat, it's solid, and it's mine. Next step is the base.
