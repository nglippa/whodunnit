On 12 March 2026, Dana Whitfield told the council that the Elm Street bridge repair would cost $1.4 million, up from the $1.1 million estimate in October. "We found corrosion in two of the four bearings," she said, "and we are not willing to reopen the bridge until both are replaced."

The work is scheduled for 18 May to 30 June. The detour via Mill Road adds about 7 minutes for most drivers. Residents can read the engineering report at https://example.org/elm-street/report-2026.pdf and send questions to Councillor Omar Haddad before 1 April.
