Personal data collected through the registration form is retained for the duration of the account and for twelve months after its closure. After that period, the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymised, except where a longer retention period is required by law.

Account holders may request a copy of their data, or its correction or deletion, by writing to the data protection officer. Requests are answered within one month of receipt. Where a request is refused, the response states the reason and the account holder's right to complain to the supervisory authority.
