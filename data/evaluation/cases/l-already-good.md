We moved the tomatoes to the south bed this year, and the difference was obvious by July. The plants in last year's spot, against the fence, never got more than five hours of sun; the new bed gets closer to nine. We picked the first ripe fruit on 2 July, almost three weeks earlier than before.

The one thing we would change is spacing. We planted them 40 centimetres apart to fit twelve plants, and by August the middle of the row was a damp tangle where blight took hold. Next year we will plant eight, 60 centimetres apart, and prune to two stems.
