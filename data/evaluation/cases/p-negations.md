The update does not delete your files. It does not change your password either, but it will sign you out of every device except this one. You cannot undo the sign-out.

Do not install the update on a shared computer unless you are the only person who uses the account. If you are not sure whether the update has finished, leave the computer on: switching it off during the update will not damage your files, but it will make the next start-up much slower.
