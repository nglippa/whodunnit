## Faithfulness and differential-equivalence admissibility

“Faithful” requires the exact registered client to construct each stage request under the intended account, bootstrap, configuration, model policy, and precedence, with synthetic nonexperimental input; the capture must be before model dispatch and must certify effective fields, not requested strings or a parallel builder (`POST-V3-SUCCESSOR-PREREGISTRATION.md:26-27,57-65`; `preregistration.json:179-204`). The capture branch may differ from experimental calls only through a committed **capture-only destination override**; it must leave body, model, settings, account/bootstrap branch and precedence identical, and all request bytes must match apart from the proven destination change and dynamic fields explicitly listed and hashed in the pre-draw lock (`POST-V3-SUCCESSOR-PREREGISTRATION.md:60,62,65`). Thus ordinary experimental calls may not inherit a proxy/CA/socket override, and demonstrating only selected prompt or sampling fields is insufficient.
