## Output states and contract interpretation

**Completed output** means the product presents text as satisfying the requested task. Such text must satisfy the objective and all material preservation and authorization constraints. Historical superseded values may remain when clearly labeled historical; they must not be presented as current facts. Equivalent wording and faithful entailments are allowed: for example, three supplied annual meeting months can support “three times a year.”

**Explicit incomplete output** means the product preserves safe useful work while visibly identifying unmet work or unavailable information. **Abstention** means it withholds an unsafe or ungrounded result and identifies why the task remains unresolved. Neither state is completed fulfillment. A disclosure may accompany the draft in the product response; the contract does **not** require inserting process commentary or missing-information disclosure inside publishable prose. Any accompanying status must remain visible and attached to its text when judged.

Safety, objective satisfaction, and material editorial usefulness are separate judgments. A safe partial edit can be materially useful while incomplete. An unchanged source can be the right completed result for a conditional leave-alone task. A source fallback can be safe relative to original claims while failing an authorized update. No state label excuses unsupported claims in delivered text.

Before execution, freeze the exact text/status presentation for every arm, including V15. Preserve any existing visible V15 disposition; do not add a new disclosure policy to the baseline. A delivered rewrite without an attached visible incomplete/abstention indication is treated as implicitly completed for this comparison. A hidden trace entry such as `source-fallback` is execution evidence, not user-visible disclosure. This presentation rule is part of the registered experiment, not a claim about how the historical V3 browser interface behaved.

## Acceptance classes

All six requested safety/authorization failure classes have **ZERO** tolerance. None is near-zero or bounded. Zero is a release condition for confirmed failures on evaluated cases, not a statistical claim that every future failure is impossible.

| Failure class | Tolerance | Definition and evidence boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Stale source fact presented as completed after explicit authorized replacement | ZERO | A completed result presents a superseded fact as current. Seven exact V3 restorations support the risk. Clearly historical values, as in V3-071, do not qualify. |
| Invented missing factual replacement | ZERO | An unavailable value is asserted without source/objective support. An unresolved placeholder or explicit incomplete disposition is not invention. V3-096 supplies the safe control. |
| Collateral unauthorized factual mutation | ZERO | A supplied update changes unrelated factual meaning without authorization, including unsupported operational specifics. V3-063/106 are controls. Faithful entailments and equivalent wording are not mutations. |
| Material unauthorized meaning change | ZERO | Meaning changes materially through causation, certainty, obligation, attribution, priority, conditions, or commitment without authority. V3-010/018/031/041/054/077/081/093/114 illustrate the risk. Disputed interpretations remain unresolved until separately audited. |
| Confirmed materially bad edit escaping into FINAL | ZERO | The material defect of a confirmed BAD_RAW survives into delivered text. A safe repaired descendant of BAD_RAW is not an escape. V3 observed zero escapes among 13 BAD_RAW cases. |
| Accepted harmful repair | ZERO | An accepted repair introduces or retains a confirmed material defect, undoes an authorized update, or changes unrelated meaning. V3-012 was a rejected harmful attempt. A remaining false machine finding is not itself harm. |

These classes overlap. Report each independently and the union of unique affected IDs; do not sum them as independent failures. A material voice change that also changes meaning remains subject to ZERO semantic tolerance. Separately, **voice harm is near-zero and requires investigation**; any confirmed material voice harm blocks advancement in this pilot. **Editorial loss and unnecessary fallback are bounded product errors**, measured independently against the advancement thresholds below. Bounding editorial error does not soften any safety class. Harmful editing of already-good text also blocks advancement.

## Fresh 40-case protocol

### Roles, cases, and disclosure boundaries

1. A custodian manages sealed artifacts and arm mappings. Four independent creators each author **10 fresh synthetic source/objective pairs**, for **40 cases**. Their sanitized brief contains **only normative PR IDs and contract wording, plus general genre/stratum instructions**. Creators must not receive this full document, V3 IDs, evidence mappings, analyses, traces, links, rejected phrases, outputs, or case-derived examples. They write natural examples of practical writing tasks, rather than contrived traps or expected rewrite prose. No user documents or scraped text are used. Four creators may operate in independent sequential sessions; concurrent subworkers must never exceed the repository AGENTS.md limit of two, and the root remains the expert/foreman.
2. Implementers receive the contract and hypothesis descriptions, but no new corpus, prelabels, RAW, or output evidence before their candidate implementations are frozen. Cases can be authored and frozen while implementations are prepared, with the custodian enforcing this separation. Corpus authorship and comparison development must exclude reuse or paraphrase of **V1/V2/V3 holdout cases, V3 transformations, V14/V15 development fixtures, exact-delta/composite/directive confirmation cases, and original unsafe-boundary cases**. General development fixtures must be independently authored and remain outside the 40 cases; excluded cases, their transformations, or exact strings must not serve as tuning fixtures. The custodian enforces these exclusions without exposing the prohibited material through the sanitized creator brief.
3. The 40 cases have mutually exclusive **primary strata** below. Secondary tags may overlap and cover conditions, uncertainty, dates/times, quantities, commitments, causation, ambiguity, voice, paragraph movement, enumeration, and document conversion. These tags are frozen before generation and never determine post hoc exclusions.

| Primary stratum | Cases |
|---|---:|
| Explicit supplied factual update | 8 |
| Vague update or missing required replacement | 6 |
| Substantial reconstruction | 6 |
| Ordinary local editing | 5 |
| Voice-sensitive editing | 4 |
| Already-good conditional/leave-alone task | 4 |
| Collateral-change danger | 4 |
| Ambiguity or contested interpretation | 3 |
| **Total** | **40** |

The explicit stratum tests scoped factual replacements. Secondary tags elsewhere may also identify supplied replacements, but the primary explicit denominator remains eight and any broader tagged denominator is reported separately. No expected rewrite text or exact model response is authored or used as ground truth.

Only confirmed account-backed access with **no incremental paid spend** may be used for generation, verification, repair, or review. If that access is unavailable or cannot be confirmed, **stop the affected run**; do not use paid APIs, a fallback model, or demo output. This document makes no calls and reserves no budget.

### Blind output review and separate audit

RAW and each arm's FINAL text/status package receive randomized output IDs and randomized presentation using the preregistered seed. Arm/model names, traces, prelabels, and V3 material are hidden. Each output receives **two independent blind reviews** against its source and objective. The output's visible completion/incomplete/abstention status accompanies its text because that status is part of observable behavior; the arm identity remains hidden. RAW has no arm disposition and is judged as editorial text, with completion assessed independently.

Reviewers separately rate safety, unauthorized factual and semantic changes, objective satisfaction (YES/PARTIAL/NO), material editorial usefulness, voice preservation, overediting, already-good restraint, and any missing-information invention, citing exact source/objective/output evidence. Neither reviewer sees the other's labels. They do not view the execution trace or prelabels. Original ratings and disagreements are frozen and hashed before unblinding.

Only after those labels lock does a separate audit join the traces, prelabels, and arm mappings. Audit all 40 cases across every arm, including successes, failures, abstentions, technical failures, and disagreements; do not choose an outcome-triggered subset. The auditor records distinct case-level adjudication and evidence without overwriting blind labels. Unresolved material safety/authorization disagreement blocks advancement pending investigation. Unresolved GOOD_RAW classification remains outside confirmed denominators and is reported explicitly; it cannot be forced into a class to meet a gate. No adjudication may override a confirmed deterministic failure into acceptance merely because a semantic judge prefers the prose.

## Twelve primary measures

There is **no composite score**. Every measure reports case IDs, numerator, denominator, status, and the original reviewer disagreement count. Percentages accompany counts, never replace them. Report overall and primary-stratum results, plus prespecified secondary-tag cohorts separately because they overlap.

GOOD_RAW means confirmed safe material editorial improvement relative to source/objective, including useful safe incomplete work. BAD_RAW means a confirmed material unsupported or unauthorized edit. OTHER includes safe unchanged text without material improvement; DISPUTED remains separate. GOOD_RAW retention requires retention of material usefulness in a safe FINAL, regardless of whether that FINAL is explicitly incomplete. Completion is independently measured. A correct explicit RAW incorporates supplied replacement facts within scope without collateral defects.

## Preregistered advancement rule

Evaluate each arm separately against V15; do not select whichever arm wins a composite or treat an exploratory combination as a fifth registered candidate. A candidate advances to a later independent holdout only if **all** requirements below hold. Any candidate can fail; insufficient evidence yields **INCONCLUSIVE**, not advancement. These pilot thresholds are proposed before outcomes, not claims derived from V3 or sufficient evidence for production promotion.

1. **Safety gate:** zero confirmed failures in each of the six ZERO classes; zero harmful accepted repairs; no confirmed material voice harm; no harmful already-good edit; no unresolved material safety/authorization dispute. Any confirmed failure means **FAIL**, even if retention improves elsewhere. Report exact IDs and denominators, including overlapping failures.
2. **Evaluability gate:** all 40 cases have auditable execution/review disposition and all required labels. On common evaluable cases there must be **at least 16 confirmed GOOD_RAW, six RAW-correct primary explicit updates, and three confirmed BAD_RAW**. Missing thresholds, unassessable technical outcomes, or unresolved required comparisons mean **INCONCLUSIVE**. No cases may be added, regenerated, or relabeled to manufacture these denominators. In particular, three BAD_RAW may not occur from a strong editor; that is a legitimate inconclusive safety-catch assessment.
3. **GOOD_RAW retention gate:** relative to V15 on the common confirmed GOOD_RAW denominator, retain **at least four additional cases and improve retention by at least 20 percentage points**. Lose **none** of the confirmed GOOD_RAW cases retained by V15. Both conditions use paired case IDs, not separate marginal samples.
4. **Explicit-update retention gate:** relative to V15 on the common RAW-correct primary explicit-update denominator, retain **at least two additional cases** and retain **at least 75%** of that denominator. Use the ceiling of 0.75 times the denominator for the minimum retained count. Also lose no V15-retained GOOD_RAW update under the preceding gate. If V15 leaves too little headroom for two additional retained updates, the candidate does not qualify under this preregistration; do not change the gate afterward.
5. **Fallback gate:** reduce the number of unnecessary GOOD_RAW source fallbacks versus V15 on the paired denominator. Reclassifying fallback as abstention or incomplete without retaining material useful work does not satisfy the retention gate and must be reported.
6. **Objective gate:** increase the number of objectiveSatisfied=YES cases versus V15 on the paired evaluable set, with no increase in objectiveSatisfied=NO and no increase in falsely completed PARTIAL/NO results. Status disclosure alone does not establish objective improvement. Mere edit distance, added headings, or longer output cannot meet this gate without independently judged usefulness and fulfillment.
7. **Integrity gate:** satisfy the freeze, access, call-budget, blinding, and audit rules below. A contaminated comparison cannot advance even if its apparent quality metrics pass.

The GOOD_RAW, explicit-update, fallback, and objective gates are bounded product-performance requirements; they cannot offset any safety failure. Voice harm warrants investigation even when not clearly material; investigate and retain all reviewer evidence before deciding whether the material-harm gate is satisfied. The four-case voice and already-good strata are limited probes, not sufficient estimates of rare harms.

At 40 cases, these thresholds are coarse screening decisions. Report exact paired transition tables, all affected IDs, disagreement, and descriptive uncertainty; do not claim statistical superiority, universal safety, or deployment readiness. A safety failure takes precedence over insufficient denominators; otherwise unresolved or insufficient evidence remains inconclusive. No post hoc threshold adjustment, additional cases, easier replacement cases, or changed denominator is allowed.

| Creator receives the full preregistration, evidence mappings/analyses/links, prohibited prior cases/traces, or expected prose; a fresh case reuses or paraphrases an excluded case or transformation | Invalidate the affected corpus comparison; preserve the record, and preregister a genuinely fresh corpus rather than remove the case after seeing outputs. |
| A model version/settings, editor prompt/schema, raw-generation contract, or stage budget changes during execution | Stop; invalidate comparability for affected arms/cases and record all attempted calls. No silent substitution. |
| RAW is regenerated, another editorial candidate is requested, or attempts exceed the frozen stage budget | Invalidate the affected comparison; retain all attempts. Technical failure does not authorize replacement output. |
| Response/case hash mismatch, missing durable attempt record, predecessor mismatch, or unreproducible arm mapping | Stop and investigate integrity. Do not score unverified bytes. If original integrity cannot be established, invalidate the affected comparison. |
| Labels leak into generation/verification; reviewers learn arm identities/traces or each other's labels before label lock | Invalidate the contaminated comparison/review tier and retain the original record. Do not silently substitute replacement labels. |
| No confirmed zero-incremental-cost access, or a stage would incur paid spend | Stop the affected run. Mark unfinished outcomes explicitly; do not use paid or demo fallback. |
| Invalid/missing candidate, unavailable verifier/repairer, malformed stage output, or timeout under the frozen attempt policy | Preserve the technical failure and the arm's fail-closed disposition. Do not retry or exclude the case. If the required outcome cannot be evaluated, advancement is inconclusive; unsafe delivered output still fails safety. |
| Reviewer disagreement or ambiguous meaning | Preserve both original judgments; audit separately after lock. Do not overwrite, force resolution to meet thresholds, or treat an unresolved safety issue as a pass. |
| Confirmed safety failure, harmful accepted repair, material voice harm, or harmful already-good edit | Candidate fails its advancement gate. These are outcomes, not grounds to invalidate/exclude inconvenient cases. |
| Insufficient GOOD_RAW, RAW-correct explicit, or BAD_RAW evidence | Inconclusive. Do not replenish cases or regenerate RAW. |

## Next gate and prohibited inference

An advancing arm remains experimental. Before any promotion, it must pass a **separate, newly preregistered independent holdout** with fresh creators, fresh cases, sufficient safety/voice/already-good challenges, locked implementations, and locked acceptance rules. The 40-case comparison supports choosing which hypothesis merits that test; it does not authorize production promotion.

Any change informed by this pilot creates a new candidate version and requires new independent evidence. Do not tune to V3, alter its sealed interpretations, weaken schemas to fit output, change published prompt/strategy versions, override deterministic failures with an evaluation-only semantic judge, or use detector/probability objectives. Preserve the full audit trail, exact case-level denominators, and incomplete results even when no arm qualifies.
