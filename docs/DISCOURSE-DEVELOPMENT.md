# Experimental discourse intelligence

This pass adds document-level **editing evidence** beside the existing local writing rules. It does not infer authorship or assign an AI score. Production remains `reconstruction-v1`; the new planner behavior is isolated in experimental `reconstruction-v5`. No cloud or local model was run for this work.

## Development material

`data/fixtures/discourse-development/` contains 108 newly authored synthetic documents in 54 clean/problematic pairs. Three independent corpus tasks produced 40 short genre documents, 40 discourse-focused documents, 20 documents of 284–320 words, and eight documents of 656–792 words. The four files are ordinary development data, **not a holdout**. The frozen historical holdout was not read or run during this pass.

| 100–299 | 30 |
| 300–599 | 28 |

There are 54 `LEAVE_ALONE`, 43 `LIGHT_EDIT`, and 11 `SUBSTANTIVE_RECONSTRUCTION` labels. Genres are prose (28), procedure (12), notes (10), policy (10), interview (10), email (8), FAQ (8), mixed (8), chat (6), transcript (4), and list (4). Pairing holds the facts largely constant while changing framing or register. Many light-edit cases insert only one weak sentence into otherwise good prose; they are intentionally hard negatives for a *distributed* detector.

Run `pnpm eval:discourse-development` for deterministic-only counts and timing. The CLI validates the corpus and emits counts, rates, and durations without source text. It never loads the frozen holdout. Its label-to-finding mapping is conceptual: a `GENERIC_REGISTER` finding covers a labeled genericness or register-cluster issue. This is a development measurement, not independent evidence of generalization.

## Evidence and action

The document classifier recognizes explicit mail headers, speaker turns, Q/A blocks, timestamps, numbered steps, bullets, and note headings. It records confidence and returns `UNKNOWN` when structure is not clear. At high confidence, genre explains narrow local form patterns such as repeated chat speaker openings or list-marker dashes. Permissions require **every match** of a rule to fit its structural scope; if one unrelated prose match appears, the rule remains active. Candidate post-checks reclassify the candidate and recheck the scope, so a source permission cannot blindly carry across a format change. Semantic safety rules are never suppressed. A prose procedure or unmarked policy can still be `PROSE` or `UNKNOWN`.

The discourse analyzer masks quotes, code, and URLs and ignores list/heading blocks for prose rhythm. It requires at least 100 words, six prose sentences, and two prose paragraphs for distributed inference. It looks for repeated **combinations** of broad nouns, broad predicates, evaluative modifiers, or nominalized forms across paragraphs. A single abstract term, a suffix count, lack of numbers, or similar paragraph lengths cannot demand a rewrite. Concrete numbers and dates are reported as protected context, never as a whole-document exemption.

`GENERIC_REGISTER` can request a distributed light edit only after at least five framed sentences across three paragraphs and a sustained share of the prose. Lesser evidence stays advisory. Structured documents, formal requested style, and a confident formal Voiceprint also keep it advisory. `POSSIBLE_RESTATEMENT` uses lexical overlap plus an abstract follow-up only as advisory evidence; shared words do not prove redundant meaning. `MECHANICAL_STRUCTURE` requires regular paragraph counts plus broad framing and is also advisory. Correlated observations never combine into automatic substantial reconstruction. Full semantic restatement and rhetorical-function equivalence remain model-assisted judgments for a future bounded review contract; there is no cloud call here.

Source-local deliberate repetition is recorded as counterevidence for structural or restatement observations. It does not automatically excuse independently sustained abstract framing: a writer can use repetition intentionally and still bury facts in generic language. Only evidence tied to the actual phenomenon changes the action.

If a later experiment adds semantic review, a single bounded `DISCOURSE_REVIEW` task should receive only the two candidate adjacent spans, their paragraph context, document type, and relevant voice counterevidence. Its strict result should classify `ADDS_INFORMATION`, `LIKELY_RESTATEMENT`, or `UNCERTAIN`, cite exact input spans, and contain no replacement prose. Missing, malformed, or unsupported evidence becomes `UNCERTAIN`; the deterministic planner and meaning verifier retain authority. This is a **proposed contract**, not an agent role or code path in this pass. Evaluating coherence, a writer's intended emphasis, and whether abstract framing genuinely adds information still requires human or model judgment.

The v5 `RewritePlan` records structure, observations, counterevidence, findings, and one of `UNCHANGED`, `LOCAL_EDIT`, `DISTRIBUTED_LIGHT_EDIT`, or `SUBSTANTIVE_RECONSTRUCTION`. The current deterministic discourse detector can produce only distributed light edits; substantial reconstruction may still come from existing local pattern intensity. The v5 prompt names the distributed editing reason and affected paragraphs, instructs the model to keep facts and author habits, and retains independent meaning verification. V1–v4 prompts and behavior stay pinned.

## Development result and limits

The development run found six discourse findings, all on labeled problematic documents: 0/54 clean, 1/43 light-edit, and 5/11 substantive documents. Four were actionable and two advisory. Thus observed finding precision on this *development set* is 6/6 and problematic document coverage is 6/54. This is a small sample of detections, so 6/6 is not a generalization estimate. No redundancy or mechanical-structure finding fired in these documents. The v5 planner left 54/54 clean documents unchanged, chose light edit for 4/43 light-edit documents and 4/11 substantive documents, and chose substantive reconstruction for none. It left 39/43 light-edit and 7/11 substantive documents unchanged. The exact disposition matrix is emitted by the CLI.

The structure classifier matched 53/108 labeled genres overall. It assigned confidence ≥0.8 to 29/108 and matched all 29; this is high precision at limited coverage, not broad genre understanding. In particular, it recognized 0/10 unmarked policy documents as policy, 2/12 procedures, and 1/4 transcripts. Other cases fall back to `PROSE` with lower confidence or `UNKNOWN`. The paired ordering improved in 8/54 planner pairs. These numbers expose the primary remaining weakness: **low recall for subtle, isolated, semantic, or genre-embedded problems**. They do not justify lowering evidence requirements until a fresh independent evaluation measures the tradeoff.

For context, deterministic v3 planning on this same development set would leave 41/54 clean documents unchanged and mark 12/43 light-edit cases for editing. V5 suppresses many structurally explained local triggers, such as chat speaker prefixes and list dashes; the drop in light-edit marks is mostly removal of *irrelevant proxies*, not proof of worse editing. Neither planner was given a model call here, so no reconstruction quality is measured.

One standalone local run of discourse analysis **plus full deterministic planning** measured 1.35 ms median / 3.42 ms p95 under 100 engine-counted words, 1.78 / 3.48 ms at 100–299, and 3.86 / 7.74 ms at 300+ (worst long document 8.74 ms). The under-100 worst was 182.27 ms on the first case because the run also initialized the rule registry; it is a cold-start observation, not per-document steady-state cost. There is no comparable pre-change performance baseline, so this does not establish a regression or speedup.

An independent read-only red team found a genuine false positive in fresh factual academic prose under the Natural preset, a transcript case where enough broad terms overrode genre counterevidence, and ambiguous Q/A and numbered arguments receiving overconfident genres. The fixes keep moderate lexical evidence advisory, retain transcript protection regardless of raw count, and classify ambiguous long Q/A or non-imperative numbering as `UNKNOWN`. Regression tests use newly authored examples, not the frozen holdout. The red team also recommended a short-text detector; that recommendation was deferred because the same review demonstrated the short-text uncertainty boundary is working, and a new local signal needs separate clean negatives before activation. Low recall and lack of substantive discourse action remain explicit limitations.

An independent final diff review found whole-rule genre permissions that could conceal unrelated matches or persist into candidate checks. Those permissions now require all matches to fit a structural scope, and the candidate's genre is checked again. It also found that pair-order reporting could count same-label ties as successes; ties are now reported separately. Its suggestion to make any source-local repetition suppress generic framing was rejected: repetition and generic framing are different phenomena, and a repetition habit is recorded without licensing abstract filler. This boundary is stated and tested explicitly.

Research informed boundaries rather than copied rule text. [Purdue OWL on concision](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/conciseness/index.html) supports removing words that add no work but does not make repetition automatically bad. [Purdue OWL on sentence clarity](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/mechanics/sentence_clarity.html) and [CDC's clear-writing assessment](https://www.cdc.gov/nceh/clearwriting/docs/Clear_Writing_Assessment-508.pdf) motivate attention to buried actions, without treating nominalization as a ban. [Mann and Thompson's rhetorical structure work](https://www.sfu.ca/rst/pdfs/Mann_Thompson_1987.pdf) distinguishes relations between spans from mere lexical overlap. [Covington and McFall on moving-average type-token ratio](https://doi.org/10.1080/09296171003643098) reinforces length sensitivity, so lexical diversity is deliberately omitted. See [DISCOURSE-RESEARCH.md](./DISCOURSE-RESEARCH.md) for the research audit and limits.

## Holdout V2 protocol (schema only; no corpus created)

Holdout V2 should be authored in a **separate session** by corpus creators who cannot inspect this implementation, thresholds, development documents, or its outputs. Target 80–120 newly authored or clearly licensed documents, with balanced clean/problematic/mixed cases and multiple lengths. Include discourse genericness, redundancy, mechanical form, legitimate formal and business language, policy/procedure, transcripts/interviews, list-heavy material, author habits, and short-text uncertainty. Label document disposition and concept-level phenomena independently; record genre and a short rationale. Obtain blind second review on a substantial sample and preserve disagreements as `AMBIGUOUS`.

The future corpus record should contain `id`, `genre`, `lengthBand`, `text`, `provenance`, and `creatorRole`. A separate gold-label record keyed by `id` should contain `disposition`, `phenomena`, `rationale`, and optional `reviewerLabel`/`ambiguity`. Freeze content and labels with separate SHA-256 fingerprints, version and date. Keep the gold-label file entirely outside reconstruction input. Run the unchanged v5 analyzer once after freezing, report detection, planning, voice, false positives, and timing separately, and never iterate on V2 examples. Do not use previous Whodunnit user drafts or send the corpus to external APIs without authorization.

Local-model benchmarking remains **PAUSED UNTIL 64 GB M5 PRO ENVIRONMENT**. The next product decision should use Holdout V2 before any model benchmark or promotion.
