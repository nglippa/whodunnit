# Astra control benchmark: independent local design audits

**Experiment:** `astra-control-fresh-48-v1`
**Scope:** preregistration design, machine protocol, Astra editor instruction, and their prospective gates. Four separate local, read-only reviewers examined the design. No repository material was sent to an external reviewer. These are **design audits**, not certifications of an authored corpus, prelabels, model entitlement, live callers, runtime behavior, output quality, or safety results. No case, arm output, model call, or outcome judgment existed for these reviews.

The four reviewers were assigned separate questions. A reviewed corpus freshness and leakage; B reviewed arm fairness and the Astra baseline; C reviewed safety and advancement; D reviewed experimental integrity and reproducibility. Each initial design concern below was addressed in the preregistration and machine protocol before any outcome existed. `PASS` means the **design** satisfies that review's current question with the future gates preserved; it does not mean generation is authorized. The registration remains `PREREGISTERED — BLOCKED BEFORE CASE CREATION AND OUTCOMES`.

## A — Corpus freshness and leakage

**A1. Historical and peer overlap.** **FACT:** The first draft named broad exclusion classes without a resolved inventory or fixed screening operation. **INTERPRETATION:** An unlisted prior case, transformed example, or near-duplicate could enter a purportedly fresh confirmatory corpus. **ASSUMPTION:** A custodian can resolve the excluded case-bearing inventory from committed history and keep it inaccessible to creators. **SEVERITY:** High in the initial draft; a blocking future implementation gate remains. **FIX / RECHECK:** The protocol now requires a committed, integrity-verified exclusion manifest before creator briefing, with canonical path, exact file SHA-256, Git blob ID, content class, and exclusion basis. It fixes NFC/full-casefold/whitespace normalization, six-token overlap, all 1,128 unordered peer pairs, independent semantic scenario and transformation review, and preservation of rejected hashes and disagreements. A's design recheck is PASS; no corpus or manifest has yet been certified.

**A2. Creator exposure and coverage.** **FACT:** Freshness depends on who authors cases and what examples they see; generic category targets do not enforce balanced coverage. **INTERPRETATION:** Creator contamination or a thin case mix could make apparent improvement uninformative. **ASSUMPTION:** Four eligible independent creators can be briefed in isolated workspaces after prior-access attestations. **SEVERITY:** High for leakage, medium for coverage in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** The protocol fixes four creators × 12 cases, eight mutually exclusive primary strata, numeric orthogonal and length floors, a sanitized brief without old examples or expected rewrites, and no creator access to excluded material or peer drafts. A's design recheck is PASS; actual creator eligibility and coverage must still be verified before corpus lock.

## B — Arm fairness and Astra baseline

**B1. Comparable Whodunnit inputs.** **FACT:** V15 and C share one saved V15 editor RAW, while Astra makes an independent direct edit; the first draft did not make every profile choice and interpretation explicit. **INTERPRETATION:** Case-specific profiles or an overclaimed control-layer comparison would add avoidable degrees of freedom. **ASSUMPTION:** The frozen V15 and C implementations can run with `PRESETS.natural` on every case through a compliant runtime. **SEVERITY:** High in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** Every V15/C case now uses exactly `PRESETS.natural`, with no refinement or voiceprint exception; source, objective, profile and RAW are identical for their downstream pair. V15 is labeled as experimental end-to-end V15, C as downstream C on its exact RAW, and Astra as an independent editor. The Astra gap is explicitly a whole-system product comparison. B's design recheck is PASS; the live caller remains blocked.

**B2. Blind product status.** **FACT:** Status and disclosure can reveal a controlled arm before a reviewer rates its text. **INTERPRETATION:** Exposing these fields in the primary packet could bias writing-quality preference. **ASSUMPTION:** The custodian can lock primary labels before releasing a separate product-package tier. **SEVERITY:** Medium in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** The primary packet contains only opaque ID, identical source/objective, and output text; five pairwise packets are also text-only with independently randomized orientation. Actual status/disclosure is presented only after original primary labels lock, and secondary product-package judgments lock before unblinding. B's design recheck is PASS. SOURCE identity and identical texts may still partly reveal arms and must be reported.

## C — Safety and advancement

**C1. RAW and repair mechanism.** **FACT:** A good-edit retention claim requires independent classification of the shared editor RAW before judges inspect FINAL or traces. **INTERPRETATION:** Without blind GOOD/BAD/MIXED/OTHER/DISPUTED labels and material spans, case selection could hide defect escape or inflate retention. **ASSUMPTION:** Two independent human judges and a third blind adjudicator can evaluate RAW from source/objective alone. **SEVERITY:** High in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** The protocol fixes two original blind RAW labels, cited safe and defect spans, adjudication before FINAL/trace/arm mapping exposure, BAD∪MIXED defect-escape accounting, MIXED safe-span retention, and an all-48 post-join trace audit for classes 5 and 6. GOOD and MIXED retention require a beneficial RAW edit in a wholly meaning-, fact-, scope- and voice-safe FINAL with no new material harm or false completion. C's final narrow recheck was PASS on the whitespace-cleaned document and unchanged protocol hashes in the table.

**C2. Safety and pilot advancement.** **FACT:** A small pilot can appear to pass with too few GOOD or MIXED RAW opportunities, technical missingness, or unresolved material authorization disputes. **INTERPRETATION:** Positive advancement must depend on prespecified mechanism exposure and all-case evaluability, without averaging away the six zero-tolerance classes. **ASSUMPTION:** The planned 48 cases can yield the stated opportunity counts; otherwise the result is honestly inconclusive. **SEVERITY:** High in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** Positive advancement requires 48/48 evaluability; at least 16 GOOD RAW, six correct supplied-update RAW, three BAD∪MIXED RAW and three MIXED RAW; prespecified GOOD and MIXED paired gains; objective and correct-update gains; no extra false completion or already-good unnecessary edits; zero six-class failures, C material voice harm and harmful already-good edits; no unresolved material safety, authorization or RAW-span dispute; and a both-safe Astra gap with missing/unsafe cells separate. C's design recheck is PASS. These are descriptive pilot gates for a later holdout, not a safety guarantee or production approval.

## D — Experimental integrity and reproducibility

**D1. Payload custody and secrets.** **FACT:** The planned capture is at the application caller boundary; raw HTTP headers could contain credentials, and a claimed exact payload cannot silently lose semantic fields. **INTERPRETATION:** Durable evidence must retain exact approved semantic payload bytes and hashes while excluding credentials. **ASSUMPTION:** Synthetic cases and the selected callers can expose the application-level request arguments and returned content without capturing transport auth headers. **SEVERITY:** High in the initial draft. **FIX / RECHECK:** The protocol requires exact application-level semantic payload bytes and SHA-256 equality before downstream use, allowlisted nonsecret metadata, no raw Authorization/Cookie/Set-Cookie/proxy-auth headers, and technical failure if secret stripping prevents exact approved semantic persistence. D's design recheck is PASS; no caller or capture implementation has been certified.

**D2. Deviations and blocked execution.** **FACT:** The registered Astra name is a moving alias without a dated snapshot in the cited model documentation; actual entitlement and compatible live V15/C account-backed zero-incremental-cost callers have not been established. **INTERPRETATION:** Generation now would leave model/runtime identity and cost claims unverified. Leakage, retries, model changes or broken custody could invalidate the whole advancement decision. **ASSUMPTION:** An additive execution lock may establish the exact permitted runtime without changing the frozen arms or hypotheses; if it cannot, generation stops. **SEVERITY:** High and still an operational blocker. **FIX / RECHECK:** The preregistration explicitly remains blocked; no false demo classification or paid-call guard bypass is allowed. Any leakage or unauthorized retry, including hidden transport retries, invalidates whole-experiment advancement. Only prespecified single-arm technical absence or unresolved judgment may leave unaffected paired comparisons descriptive, never support positive advancement. D's design recheck is PASS; the operational blocker is unresolved.
